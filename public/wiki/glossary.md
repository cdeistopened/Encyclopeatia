# Glossary

## A

**Activated charcoal** — episodic gut binder for endotoxin spikes; episodic-use tool, not a daily staple. [[activated-charcoal]] (938w)

**ADHD** — in this framework, a downstream symptom of low energy availability, gut endotoxin, and serotonin excess — not a fixed neurotype. [[adhd]] (853w)

**Adrenaline** — sympathetic stress hormone; the first wave after blood sugar drops; chronically elevated by low-carb and PUFA-loaded tissue. [[adrenaline]] (stub — queued)

**Age-pigment (lipofuscin)** — yellow-brown intracellular debris from peroxidized PUFA + iron; marks cumulative lipid-peroxidation damage. [[age-pigment]] (3,560w)

**Alcohol** — net stressor; small doses can lower endotoxin and serotonin, but ethanol's NADH load and acetaldehyde damage dominate. [[alcohol]] (3,628w)

**Aldosterone** — salt-retaining mineralocorticoid; elevated by low-sodium diet via RAAS; cardiac-fibrosis driver. [[aldosterone]] (4,161w)

**Allopregnanolone** — 5α-reduced progesterone metabolite; GABA-A allosteric modulator; the real mechanism behind progesterone's anxiolytic effect and a key node in finasteride's neuropsychiatric harm. [[allopregnanolone]] (3,938w)

**Altitude** — chronic elevation = paradoxical longevity signal via CO₂ retention and lactate suppression, not despite the thin air but because of it. [[altitude]] (4,967w)

**Anti-stress diet** — Peat's protocol for breaking the stress cascade: sugar + salt + protein + saturated fat, eaten frequently. [[anti-stress-diet]] (7,492w)

**Anxiety/depression** — in this framework, a stress/serotonin/CRH disorder of energy production, not a brain-chemistry imbalance. [[anxiety-depression]] (5,996w)

**Apple** — modest fruit; pectin contributes some gut-binding effect; not a top-tier sugar source. [[apple]] (1,041w)

**Arachidonic acid** — 20-carbon omega-6; substrate for series-2 prostaglandins; sourced from both food and elongation of linoleic acid. [[arachidonic-acid]] (5,141w)

**Aromatase** — enzyme that converts androgens to estrogens; tissue-local upregulation explains stubborn estrogen-driven disease (fibroids, endometriosis, gynecomastia). [[aromatase]] (3,084w)

**Aspirin** — broad anti-inflammatory tool; works at three levels (COX-1/2 inhibition, lipoxin promotion, mitochondrial protection); cornerstone of Peat's pharmacy. [[aspirin]] (7,388w)

**Avocado** — high-PUFA fruit; not recommended despite cultural health-food status. [[avocado]] (1,566w)

## B

**Bag breathing** — rebreathing exhaled CO₂ to raise blood CO₂ and reverse hyperventilation; cheap diagnostic + therapeutic. [[bag-breathing]] (1,432w)

**Baking soda (sodium bicarbonate)** — alkalinizer used to buffer aspirin and reduce lactic acidosis; also a cheap CO₂ donor. [[baking-soda]] (953w)

**Bamboo shoots** — anti-serotonin, anti-endotoxin fibrous vegetable; well-cooked is the relevant form. [[bamboo-shoots]] (1,313w)

**Banana** — moderate sugar; potassium-rich; not a top-tier fruit due to starch and serotonin content. [[banana]] (1,354w)

**Beans** — high in PUFA, phytate, and endotoxin-promoting fiber; not a recommended protein source. [[beans]] (1,116w)

**Beef** — solid mainstream protein but muscle-meat-heavy; needs gelatin/dairy balance for amino-acid ratio. [[beef]] (1,015w)

**Berries** — fragile fruits with bioflavonoid content; modest sugar; pesticide load is the practical limit. [[berries]] (1,356w)

**Bioenergetics** — Peat's umbrella term for the framework: energy production as the organizer of every other physiological process. [[bioenergetics]] (6,257w)

**Bipolar** — manic-depressive cycling; in this frame, oscillation between catabolic stress states, not a fixed genetic disease. [[bipolar]] (1,248w)

**Blood sugar** — the anchor for stress prevention; defending glucose = defending the whole bioenergetic state. [[blood-sugar]] (5,359w)

**Bone broth** — gelatin-rich; balances muscle-meat tryptophan; supports gut and joints. [[bone-broth]] (1,440w)

**Borax** — boron-containing supplement used in low doses for joints and hormone balance; controversial mainstream framing. [[borax]] (1,069w)

**Broccoli** — sulforaphane and goitrogen content; not a Peat-preferred vegetable due to gas/endotoxin. [[broccoli]] (1,039w)

**Broda Barnes** — endocrinologist (1906–1988) whose thyroid-and-heart-disease work Peat built on; T3 axillary-temperature diagnostics. [[broda-barnes]] (3,768w)

**Butter** — saturated, vitamin-A-rich, low-PUFA fat; daily staple when sourced from grass-fed cows. [[butter]] (2,327w)

## C

**Calcium** — paired with phosphate in the Ca:P ratio; dairy is the framework's preferred source; not the bone-disease villain mainstream nutrition makes it. [[calcium]] (3,338w)

**Carbon dioxide** — not a waste product; structures water, stabilizes hemoglobin, suppresses lactate, anti-inflammatory; the chemistry of life. [[carbon-dioxide]] (3,500w)

**Cardiolipin** — mitochondrial inner-membrane phospholipid with four fatty-acid tails; PUFA substitution at one specific site drives mitochondrial decline. [[cardiolipin]] (3,989w)

**Carrot salad** — raw shredded carrot + vinegar + coconut oil + salt; binds endotoxin and excess estrogen; daily Peat staple. [[carrot-salad]] (1,414w)

**Cascara sagrada** — gentle laxative herb; preferred over senna or stimulant alternatives. [[cascara-sagrada]] (927w)

**Cheese** — concentrated dairy protein; high in calcium and phosphate; quality-of-source matters more than quantity. [[cheese]] (2,307w)

**Cherries** — flavonoid-rich; modest sugar; melatonin content is dietarily trivial. [[cherries]] (1,007w)

**Chicken** — high-PUFA meat by modern feeding practices; not a preferred protein. [[chicken]] (1,197w)

**Chocolate** — magnesium-rich; flavonoid + caffeine + theobromine package; quality of source matters (low cadmium). [[chocolate]] (1,575w)

**Cholesterol** — substrate for every steroid hormone; not a disease marker but a synthesis bottleneck when low. [[cholesterol]] (3,189w)

**Cocoa butter** — saturated, stable, low-PUFA fat; useful for cooking and skin. [[cocoa-butter]] (1,993w)

**Coconut oil** — highly saturated, MCT-rich, thyroid-supportive cooking fat; staple in the framework. [[coconut-oil]] (3,657w)

**Coffee** — caffeine + magnesium + niacinamide-by-roasting; protective when taken with sugar and food, harmful on an empty stomach. [[coffee]] (2,949w)

**Cold/flu** — acute infectious episode treated by raising metabolism (sugar, salt, aspirin, vitamin A) rather than suppressing fever. [[cold-flu]] (4,961w)

**Collagen** — structural protein; glycine-and-proline-rich; balances muscle-meat tryptophan when eaten as gelatin. [[collagen]] (2,310w)

**Consciousness** — anti-Cartesian framing; CO₂ as cognitive substrate; dreaming and rumination as opposite metabolic states. [[consciousness]] (6,758w)

**Copper** — paired with iron; ceruloplasmin enables iron mobilization; deficiency mimics iron-overload symptoms. [[copper]] (2,467w)

**Core diet protocol** — the foundation Peat-style eating pattern; four-staples baseline + grazing decision tree + 8-layer optimization. [[core-diet-protocol]] (6,512w)

**Cortisol** — catabolic stress hormone; rises when sugar and thyroid drop; tissue-destructive when chronic. [[cortisol]] (3,144w)

**Cream** — clean dairy fat; lower-protein-load way to add saturated fat to milk. [[cream]] (1,150w)

**CRH (corticotropin-releasing hormone)** — upstream of cortisol; mediates many stress effects directly via histamine, NO, and mast cells — not just via HPA. [[crh]] (3,469w)

## D

**Dates** — concentrated sugar; whole-food fructose source; useful in small amounts. [[dates]] (953w)

**Dementia** — brain-diabetes inversion: dementia is brain energy failure, with estrogen and PUFA-acrolein as drivers. [[dementia]] (7,003w)

**DHEA** — adrenal androgen; substrate for testosterone and estrogen; low-dose oral use is common in the framework. [[dhea]] (3,714w)

**DHT (dihydrotestosterone)** — 5α-reduced testosterone; mainstream villain of hair loss but a metabolic bodyguard; suppression by finasteride is dangerous. [[dht]] (4,383w)

**Dopamine** — alertness/anti-prolactin neurotransmitter; opposite-of-serotonin partner in mood. [[dopamine]] (3,092w)

## E

**Eggs** — complete protein + cholesterol + vitamin A; biotin balance via cooking; daily-staple food. [[eggs]] (4,111w)

**Endometriosis** — local-aromatase + prostaglandin disease of misplaced endometrial tissue; estrogen-prostaglandin positive-feedback loop. [[endometriosis]] (4,997w)

**Endotoxin** — bacterial LPS from gut; drives systemic inflammation; raised by fiber-fermenting bacteria and slowed transit. [[endotoxin]] (4,142w)

**Energy restoration protocol** — entry-level program for someone in a hypometabolic stress state; sleep + sugar + thyroid + light. [[energy-restoration]] (2,887w)

**Estrogen** — at chronic excess, pro-fibrotic, pro-cancer, anti-thyroid; not the female health hormone of pharmaceutical mythology. [[estrogen]] (8,043w)

**Estrogen detox** — protocol to lower estrogen load: progesterone, vitamin A, gut-binding fibers, xenoestrogen avoidance. [[estrogen-detox]] (3,176w)

**Exercise** — concentric/walking framing; eccentric and endurance modalities raise cortisol and lactate; "exercise more" is not the answer. [[exercise]] (5,485w)

## F

**Fibroids (uterine)** — myometrial fibrosis; local-aromatase + 4-hydroxyestradiol mutagenic pathway; shrinkable by lowering estrogen exposure. [[fibroids]] (4,498w)

**Fibrosis** — failed healing that calcifies tissue; TGF-β as master driver; reversible early, structural later. [[fibrosis]] (5,267w)

**Finasteride** — 5α-reductase inhibitor; iatrogenic disaster via allopregnanolone collapse; see post-finasteride syndrome. [[finasteride]] (stub — queued, Tier 3)

**Fish oil** — concentrated PUFA; lipid-peroxidation accelerator; ostensible health benefits are crowding-out effects from lowering other PUFA. [[fish-oil]] (4,061w)

**Free fatty acids** — released from fat tissue under stress; outcompete glucose at the mitochondrion (Randle cycle); a key disease driver. [[free-fatty-acids]] (4,354w)

**Free radicals** — reactive oxygen/nitrogen species; not always bad — controlled bursts are signaling, chronic load is damage. [[free-radicals]] (stub — pointer file, queued)

**Fructose** — fast-utilization sugar; bypasses some glycolytic regulation; smaller liver-glycogen-restoring fraction of fruit sugar. [[fructose]] (3,722w)

## G

**Gelatin** — collagen-derived protein; glycine-rich; the framework's primary anti-tryptophan balancer. [[gelatin]] (4,343w)

**Genetic determinism** — the framework rejects gene-centric biology; cytoplasm, environment, and energy state dominate genetic "blueprints." [[genetic-determinism]] (9,014w)

**Ghee** — clarified butter; saturated, lactose-free, stable for cooking. [[ghee]] (920w)

**Gilbert Ling** — cell physiologist whose Association-Induction hypothesis (cell = rechargeable battery, not membrane-pump system) underpins Peat's view of cellular water. [[gilbert-ling]] (4,719w)

**Glucose** — primary brain and stress-prevention fuel; framework defends, never restricts. [[glucose]] (5,068w)

**Glycine** — anti-inflammatory amino acid; balances muscle-meat tryptophan; calming via NMDA-glycine site and gut. [[glycine]] (2,511w)

**Grapefruit** — bitter fruit; CYP3A4 inhibitor; modest sugar; concentrated phytochemicals. [[grapefruit]] (1,472w)

**Grapes** — fragile sugar source; resveratrol mainstream story is overhyped. [[grapes]] (847w)

**Guava** — tropical fruit; high vitamin C; gut-binding pectin fiber. [[guava]] (1,702w)

## H

**Hair loss** — DHT-as-marker inversion: hair loss is a scalp metabolic-stress disease, not a DHT excess; prolactin, PTH, copper/iron all upstream. [[hair-loss]] (5,263w)

**Hans Selye** — Hungarian endocrinologist (1907–1982) who defined stress and the General Adaptation Syndrome; Peat extended his progesterone-as-anti-stress work. [[hans-selye]] (6,412w)

**Heart disease** — Broda Barnes T3-first reread; cholesterol myth; statin critique; Szent-Györgyi staircase. [[heart-disease]] (6,538w)

**Histamine** — vasodilator, gut-permeability raiser, mast-cell mediator; chronic elevation is a stress signal. [[histamine]] (2,877w)

**Honey** — concentrated sugar; raw quality varies enormously; small-amount sweetener. [[honey]] (1,214w)

**Hormesis** — the "what doesn't kill you" frame for fasting, cold plunges, exercise stress; in Peat's view, mostly a misread of compensation as benefit. [[hormesis]] (5,025w)

**Hypothyroidism** — most common and underdiagnosed endocrine disease; TSH is a poor diagnostic; temperature and pulse are better. [[hypothyroidism]] (9,280w)

**Hypothyroid recovery protocol** — Barnes-style T3 introduction + nutritional substrate + temperature tracking. [[hypothyroid-recovery]] (4,014w)

## I

**Ice cream** — milk + cream + sugar; calorie-dense recovery food; quality of source dictates value. [[ice-cream]] (1,441w)

**Immune protocol** — acute-illness response: raise temperature, sugar, salt, aspirin, vitamin A. [[immune-protocol]] (2,829w)

**Inflammation** — in this framework, failure to finish healing — not immune attack; mainstream pictures the immune system as adversary, Peat as broken repair crew. [[inflammation]] (6,461w)

**Insulin** — anabolic substrate-utilization signal; chronic elevation = fatty-acid dysregulation, not the carb-villain story. [[insulin]] (2,493w)

**Intermittent fasting** — popular but counter-productive; raises stress hormones to defend blood sugar; opposite of the framework's prescription. [[intermittent-fasting]] (4,966w)

**Iodine** — thyroid substrate; supplementation can paradoxically worsen thyroid function in excess. [[iodine]] (3,117w)

**Iron** — pro-oxidant when unbound; ceruloplasmin/copper-dependent for safe handling; phlebotomy is sometimes therapeutic. [[iron]] (4,435w)

## K

**Kale** — goitrogenic raw; not a Peat-preferred vegetable. [[kale]] (948w)

## L

**Lactic acid** — anaerobic metabolic dead-end; rises with stress, inflammation, and tumor metabolism (Warburg); a disease marker, not just exercise. [[lactic-acid]] (1,619w)

**Lamb** — moderate-PUFA red meat; better than chicken or pork, not as clean as beef. [[lamb]] (957w)

**Lard** — increasingly PUFA-loaded by modern pig feeding; quality depends on source. [[lard]] (1,066w)

**Learned helplessness** — Seligman's model reframed by Peat as a physiological stress state, not a cognitive distortion; reversible by restoring energy. [[learned-helplessness]] (5,426w)

**Linoleic acid** — the omega-6 PUFA; the dominant dietary toxin in modern diets; precursor to arachidonic acid. [[linoleic-acid]] (4,073w)

**Lipid peroxidation** — PUFA + iron + oxygen → reactive aldehydes (HNE, MDA, acrolein); the chemistry behind PUFA's harm. [[lipid-peroxidation]] (4,954w)

**Lisinopril** — ACE inhibitor; lowers angiotensin II; one of Peat's framework-compatible cardiovascular drugs (with progesterone, spironolactone). [[lisinopril]] (stub — queued, Tier 3)

**Liver (food)** — concentrated vitamin A, copper, B-vitamins; weekly small serving is the framework prescription. [[liver]] (3,725w)

## M

**Mango** — tropical fruit; gut-friendly; modest sugar. [[mango]] (910w)

**Mania** — manic phase of bipolar; in this frame, a high-cortisol/high-adrenaline burnout-bound state. [[mania]] (812w)

**Mast cells** — histamine/serotonin/TNF storehouses; chronic activation = inflammation + fibrosis amplifier. [[mast-cells]] (stub — queued, Tier 3)

**Mental health cluster** — anxiety, depression, ADHD, bipolar, schizophrenia all share the energy-failure pattern; not separable into distinct brain diseases. [[mental-health-cluster]] (1,272w)

**Mitochondria** — site of oxidative phosphorylation; cardiolipin-dependent ETC; PUFA degrades five attack vectors; the cell's energy factory and signaling hub. [[mitochondria]] (6,844w)

**Morning routine** — sugar + protein + salt + sunlight + warmth within the first hour to set the metabolic day. [[morning-routine]] (2,561w)

**Mushrooms** — well-cooked anti-endotoxin food (chitin-binding); raw form is problematic. [[mushrooms]] (1,180w)

## N

**Niacinamide** — vitamin B3 amide form; anti-lipolytic, anti-inflammatory; NAD precursor; framework staple. [[niacinamide]] (1,675w)

**Nuts** — high-PUFA mostly; macadamia is the main exception; not a recommended snack. [[nuts]] (1,477w)

## O

**Oats** — fiber-heavy starch with some PUFA; not the heart-health hero of cereal marketing. [[oats]] (1,086w)

**Olive oil** — monounsaturated; better than seed oils but worse than saturated fats; not a daily staple. [[olive-oil]] (1,237w)

**Oranges** — vitamin C + fructose + sucrose + bioflavonoids; the canonical breakfast fruit. [[oranges]] (1,999w)

**Oysters** — concentrated zinc, copper, B12; small portions for trace minerals. [[oysters]] (1,009w)

## P

**Papaya** — tropical fruit with proteolytic enzymes; modest sugar; gut-friendly. [[papaya]] (864w)

**Peatmaxxing cut** — bodybuilder/aesthetics-flavored aggressive lean-out protocol within the framework. [[peatmaxxing-cut]] (6,697w)

**Phosphate** — paired with calcium; modern diets are phosphate-excess relative to calcium; bone-pathology contributor. [[phosphate]] (4,116w)

**PMS** — luteal estrogen:progesterone collapse; Brewer-toxemia parallel; Katharina Dalton lineage; SSRI mechanism reframed via allopregnanolone. [[pms]] (4,994w)

**Pork** — high-PUFA modern meat; not recommended. [[pork]] (1,184w)

**Post-finasteride syndrome** — persistent sexual, cognitive, and mood disorder after 5α-reductase inhibition; allopregnanolone-driven mechanism. [[post-finasteride-syndrome]] (3,979w)

**Potatoes** — clean starch with potassium; well-cooked is the relevant form; framework-acceptable carbohydrate. [[potatoes]] (1,469w)

**Powdered milk** — convenient milk concentrate; oxidized cholesterol concern; quality varies. [[powdered-milk]] (1,349w)

**Pregnancy nutrition** — Brewer-Peat lineage; salt 6–12g/day, protein 120–200g/day; toxemia-prevention orientation. [[pregnancy-nutrition]] (4,102w)

**Progesterone** — anti-stress hormone original; anti-estrogen; neuroprotective via allopregnanolone; framework cornerstone. [[progesterone]] (6,860w)

**Progest-E** — vitamin-E-suspended progesterone preparation; oral/transdermal/transmucosal dosing form Peat designed. [[progest-e]] (1,136w)

**Prostaglandins** — eicosanoid signaling molecules; series 1/2/3 distinction; aspirin's three-level mechanism inhibits the inflammatory side. [[prostaglandins]] (5,893w)

**PUFA (polyunsaturated fatty acids)** — the framework's central dietary toxin; multiple double-bonds → lipid-peroxidation cascade → mitochondrial damage. [[pufa]] (8,558w)

**PUFA depletion protocol** — multi-year program to lower body-fat linoleic-acid stores via dietary substitution + caloric balance. [[pufa-depletion]] (3,751w)

**Pyruvate dehydrogenase (PDH)** — the gateway enzyme converting pyruvate → acetyl-CoA; B1/B2/B3/B5 cofactor-dependent; bottleneck for oxidative metabolism. [[pyruvate-dehydrogenase]] (5,964w)

**Randle cycle** — glucose-fatty-acid competition at the mitochondrion; free fatty acids block glucose oxidation; central to insulin resistance. [[randle-cycle]] (6,061w)

**Raw milk** — unprocessed dairy; quality + safety tradeoff; framework-acceptable when sourced well. [[raw-milk]] (1,426w)

**Red light** — 600–700nm + near-infrared; mitochondrial-stimulating; reverses some PUFA damage; cheap intervention. [[red-light]] (5,546w)

**Retine** — Szent-Györgyi's proposed cell-division-inhibitor (possibly methylglyoxal); framework-relevant cancer-mechanism candidate. [[retine]] (stub — queued, Tier 2; see [[methylglyoxal]])

**Reverse T3 (rT3)** — inactive thyroid metabolite; rises with stress; mainstream labs underweight it. [[reverse-t3]] (stub — queued, Tier 2)

**Saturated fat** — stable, low-PUFA dietary fat; framework-preferred class; mainstream demonization is misread. [[saturated-fat]] (stub — pointer file, queued)

**Serotonin** — Peat called it "the depression molecule" — gut-derived inflammatory signal that mainstream psychiatry recast as mood molecule. [[serotonin]] (3,465w)

**Shrimp** — clean lean protein; selenium, copper, B12; low-PUFA. [[shrimp]] (1,145w)

**Sleep** — restorative low-stress state; supported by sugar at bedtime, warmth, darkness; collapses under metabolic stress. [[sleep]] (8,517w)

**Soy** — anti-thyroid + estrogenic + PUFA-loaded; framework rejects across forms. [[soy]] (1,821w)

**Spinach** — oxalate-loaded; modest gain for the calcium/magnesium cost. [[spinach]] (1,090w)

**Spironolactone** — mineralocorticoid-receptor antagonist; framework-compatible aldosterone blocker. [[spironolactone]] (stub — queued, Tier 3)

**Sucrose** — table sugar; 50/50 glucose-fructose disaccharide; framework's clean concentrated sweetener. [[sucrose]] (4,121w)

**Szent-Györgyi (Albert)** — Hungarian-American Nobel laureate (1893–1986); discovered vitamin C; "electronic biology" + retine/promine + structured water — Peat's deepest intellectual ancestor. [[albert-szent-gyorgyi]] (6,054w)

## T

**Tallow** — beef fat; saturated, low-PUFA; cooking-stable. [[tallow]] (1,078w)

**Temperature tracking** — axillary AM body temperature as primary thyroid-function diagnostic; Barnes lineage. [[temperature-tracking]] (2,954w)

**Thyroid** — T3/T4 hormones from the thyroid gland; the master metabolic-rate regulator; underdiagnosed and undertreated. [[thyroid]] (9,591w)

**Tryptophan** — essential amino acid; serotonin precursor; muscle-meat excess drives serotonergic inflammation. [[tryptophan]] (4,105w)

**TSH (thyroid-stimulating hormone)** — pituitary hormone; mainstream's primary thyroid diagnostic but a lagging, unreliable indicator. [[tsh]] (3,366w)

## V

**Vitamin A (retinol)** — anti-estrogenic, pro-steroidogenic, immune-supporting; liver/eggs/dairy sources; mainstream toxicity fears are overblown. [[vitamin-a]] (3,044w)

**Vitamin B12** — energy + neurological + methylation cofactor; animal-food-dependent. [[vitamin-b12]] (1,796w)

**Vitamin D** — pro-hormone; calcium handling; immune modulation; framework-staple supplement at moderate doses. [[vitamin-d]] (3,940w)

**Vitamin E** — anti-PUFA-peroxidation; α-tocopherol the relevant form; framework-staple lipid antioxidant. [[vitamin-e]] (4,085w)

**Vitamin K** — K1 (leafy) vs K2 (animal/fermented); calcium-routing; arterial-calcification-prevention via matrix-Gla. [[vitamin-k]] (3,365w)

## W

**Watermelon** — high-water fructose fruit; summer staple; cycle-of-life shape. [[watermelon]] (1,338w)

**White rice** — clean starch; well-cooked + with-fat form is framework-acceptable. [[white-rice]] (1,276w)

**Whole wheat** — endotoxin-promoting fiber + PUFA + gluten; framework rejects in favor of white rice or potatoes. [[whole-wheat]] (1,752w)

**Wine** — alcohol + polyphenols; small amounts protective in some claims, framework treats as net stress with rare exceptions. [[wine]] (1,374w)

## X
