# Ray Peat — Timeline

*Chronological view of Ray Peat's life and intellectual arc. For the full biographical synthesis see [[ray-peat]]. For the framework's evolution and position shifts see [[where-peat-evolved]]. For the encyclopedia front door see [[README]].*

This timeline anchors the corpus in calendar time. The dates come from three sources: (1) verbatim publication dates in the 180-newsletter manifest and 48-newspaper-letter manifest, (2) book copyright pages, and (3) the transcript archive — 253 polished episodes across 14 shows, with dates encoded in filenames. Where biographical details (degrees, marriages, residences) are widely reported but not in the corpus itself, they are marked **approximate** or **verify**. The corpus alone cannot pin every life event, and the timeline is honest about that.

The shape of Peat's intellectual life: three decades of formation and writing in relative obscurity (1960s–1980s), a decade of book-length consolidation (1990s), a decade of internet-era expansion (2000s), and a decade of mass-audience emergence through radio and the Peat-online community (2010s–2022).

## Pre-1960: Origins

- **1936** — Raymond Peat born in Oregon.
- **Late 1950s (approximate, verify)** — Undergraduate years. Peat has cited Reed College in interviews; the corpus itself does not document his undergraduate degree directly.

## 1960s: Formation

- **1967** — First documented published writing: four letters to the editor (*Art and Sculpture*, *Government Deceit*, *Pacification Plan*, *War's Bad Publicity*) appear in unidentified publications. These are political letters — early evidence of Peat's lifelong willingness to write in public against institutional authority, in the same voice he would later turn on endocrinology and oncology.
- **1969** — Three more letters published, including *Program Not Experiment* (Oregon Daily Emerald, Feb 18, 1969). The Oregon Daily Emerald becomes a recurring outlet, placing Peat in or near the University of Oregon community.
- **Late 1960s (approximate)** — Begins graduate work at the University of Oregon under Arnold Soderwall, focused on age-related changes in reproductive physiology. The dissertation on aging and estrogen becomes the seed of his lifelong anti-estrogen position.

## 1970s: The early science

- **1970** — Letters *Dear Good Times* and *Project Sunshine* published.
- **June 1971** — *Response to "A New Look at the Meaning of Reality"* in *Science News* — the earliest dated piece in the newsletter manifest. Peat enters the broader science-journalism conversation.
- **September 1971** — *Cold-Inactivated Enzymes as Metabolic Controls* in *Physiological Chemistry and Physics* — the first peer-reviewed publication in the corpus.
- **April 1972** — *Estrogen Stimulated Pathway Changes and Cold-Inactivated Enzymes* in *Physiological Chemistry and Physics*. The estrogen–enzyme machinery that will dominate his later writing is already on the page.
- **~1972 (approximate)** — Receives PhD in biology from the University of Oregon. Dissertation under Arnold Soderwall on age-related estrogen and reproductive aging.
- **September 1972** — *Gas Industry's "Shortage"* in *Science News* — Peat writes on energy policy in the broader sense, anticipating decades of cross-domain commentary.
- **1972** — *Response to Tim Zell* in *Green Egg*, an early counterculture/Pagan magazine — placing Peat momentarily in the alternative-press ecosystem of the 1970s.
- **1973** — *Energy and Structure in Biological Water* in *Energy and Character* — the first explicit statement of the cell-water framework that would later draw him to [[gilbert-ling]]'s work.
- **Early-to-mid 1970s** — Mexico years begin. Peat teaches and consults in Mexico; the clinical experience with female patients on hormones builds the empirical base for *From PMS to Menopause*.
- **1975** — *A Biophysical Approach to Altered Consciousness* (Journal of Orthomolecular Psychiatry), *A Holistic Physiology of Memory* (Multidisciplinary Research), *A Revolution in Physics* (Multidisciplinary Research). Three pieces in one year, ranging from psychiatry to physics.
- **1976** — *Neutrinos and Long-Range Interactions* and *Can Some "Anomalous" Structural Interactions Be Explained by an "Excitable Ether"?* in *Multidisciplinary Research*. *Mind and Tissue: Russian Research Perspectives on the Human Brain* published — Peat's first book-length work, drawing on Soviet psychophysiology and the Vernadsky / Ling lineage. Re-published in 1994 with the same Eugene PO Box address.
- **Late 1970s** — Returns to Oregon; settles in Eugene, where he will remain for the rest of his life.

## 1980s: The newsletter institution

The 1980s is when Peat's framework consolidates into the form readers recognize today: anti-PUFA, anti-estrogen, pro-progesterone, pro-thyroid, pro-CO2. The vehicle is the *Townsend Letter for Doctors*, a clinician-facing monthly that publishes him regularly from 1983 through the late 1990s.

- **1980** — *Heredity's Hot Debate* in *Science News* (August 30, 1980) and *More on Vitamin C* (letter).
- **November/December 1983** — *Transdermal Progesterone for Premenstrual Syndrome* in *Townsend Letter for Doctors* — the start of the long *Townsend Letter* run that becomes the backbone of his audience among clinicians.
- **1983** — *Racism and Ideology* (Eugene Register-Guard, Oct 27); *Which Revolution?* (Eugene Register-Guard, May 30); *Under U.S. Control* (Eugene Register-Guard, Aug 2). The Eugene Register-Guard becomes the local-letter outlet that runs alongside the *Townsend Letter* national writing.
- **1984** — *Response to Recent Pop-Medicine Articles on Progesterone Therapy* (*Townsend Letter*) — Peat begins publicly correcting other writers on hormone therapy.
- **September 1984** — *Education as Advertised in Napoleonic Times* in *The Suffolk Review*.
- **April 1985** — *Darkness, Water, Osteoporosis* in *Townsend Letter for Doctors* — early version of the bone-density-as-tissue-renewal framework that will recur for the next 30 years.
- **August 1985** — *Energy, Entropy and Estrogens in Aging* in *Rejuvenation* — the explicit estrogen-as-entropy framing that becomes a Peat signature.
- **October 1985** — *Immunity, Hormones and Yeast Infections* and *Vitamin A Toxicity* (both *Townsend Letter*).
- **1986** — *Coconut Oil* article published on raypeat.com (date approximate; the raypeat.com archive does not date it precisely but it is widely cited as a mid-1980s essay — **verify**). *Asthma and Metabisulfite* (June 1986, *Townsend Letter*); *Hormone Balancing: Natural Treatment and Cure for Arthritis* (*Journal of the Rheumatoid Disease Foundation*); *Iodine Injections* (October 1986, *Townsend Letter*); *Oral Absorption of Progesterone* (*JAMA*).
- **January 1987** — *The Progesterone Deceptions* in *Ray Peat's Newsletter* — the consolidation of the progesterone case, with explicit critique of the commercial and regulatory environment around hormone therapy.
- **July 1987** — *Rosacea, Rhynophyma, Pterygium & Riboflavin* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **January 1988** — *Abnormal Cells in the Uterine Cervix* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **May 1988** — *Unsaturated Oils Have Deleterious Effects* in *Townsend Letter for Doctors* — the first explicit, public anti-PUFA statement under that direct title in the dated corpus. (The thinking is older — present in the 1985–86 essays — but this is where the position becomes a headline.)
- **June 1988** — *AIDS/Syphilis Treatments & Ideas* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **August/September 1988** — *Natural Immunity and Viral Infections* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **January 1989** — *A New Approach to Detoxifying* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **February/March 1989** — *Sales Pitch for Linseed Oil* (*Townsend Letter*) — anti-PUFA position now extended explicitly to flax oil.
- **May 1989** — *The Edema of Stress* in *Townsend Letter for Doctors* — the progesterone-as-aldosterone-antagonist framework consolidated.
- **August/September 1989** — *Szent-Györgyi's Energy* (*Townsend Letter*) — explicit canonization of [[albert-szent-gyorgyi]] as intellectual ancestor.
- **November 1989** — *Repairing Immunodeficiencies* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **1989 (approximate)** — Pre-internet subscriber base for *Ray Peat's Newsletter* (the self-published serial, distinct from *Townsend Letter* republications) is in place. Subscribers receive issues by post; back-issues circulate among clinicians.

## 1990s: The books

The 1990s is when Peat publishes the two books that anchor his public reputation: *Generative Energy* (1994) and *From PMS to Menopause* (1997). The *Townsend Letter* writing continues throughout.

- **August/September 1990** — *On Recycling Placentas, Thyroid Suppression, and Carpal Tunnel* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **October 1990** — *Aspects of Wholeness* (*Townsend Letter*) — proto-version of *Generative Energy*'s opening chapter.
- **December 1990** — *Education Against Knowledge* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **January 1991** — *Solving Some of the Problems of Aging* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **February/March 1991** — *Bowel Toxins Accelerate Aging Process* — endotoxin and serotonin are now explicit themes.
- **April 1991** — *Estrogen in 1990* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **June 1991** — *Biological Balance and Addictions* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **July 1991** — *Sharks, Salmon & Osmotic Therapies* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **1991** — *The Generality of Adaptogens* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **February 21, 1991** — *Ill-Informed Statement* (Eugene Register-Guard) — last newspaper letter in the 48-letter archive.
- **February/March 1992** — *Taxol, Yew & Cancer* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **June 1992** — *Resistance of the Heart* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **August/September 1992** — *Brain Aging* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **November 1992** — *Origins of Progesterone Therapy* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **December 1992** — *Failures of Immunity* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **1993** — *The Bean Syndrome* in *Townsend Letter for Doctors* — explicit warning on legume lectins and the broader fiber/plant-toxin theme.
- **November 1993** — *Thyroid: Misconceptions* (*Townsend Letter*) — Peat's clinical thyroid framework consolidated.
- **1993** — *Multiple Sclerosis and Other Hormone-Related Brain Syndromes* (*Ray Peat's Newsletter*).
- **April 1994** — *Insomnia & Hyperactivity* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **June 1994** — *Mortality Again* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **July 1994** — *Chronic Fatigue* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **November 1994** — *Criticism and the Experts* and *Progesterone Can Be Taken Orally* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **1994** — ***Generative Energy: Restoring the Wholeness of Life*** published. The book is the first integrated statement of the framework — Aspects of Wholeness, Vernadsky's holistic science, the centrality of anticipation, steroids, thyroid, the stress of darkness, the rainforest of the Pacific Northwest. Self-published from Peat's Eugene PO Box (5764). Second edition (also 1994) in the corpus.
- **February/March 1995** — *Oral Progesterone is Not Inactivated by Stomach Acids, Pancreatic Enzymes or Liver Detoxification* (*Townsend Letter*) — the bioavailability case for oral progesterone made explicit, against transdermal-only commercial preparations.
- **August/September 1995** — *Aging Ovaries: Not the Eggs* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **November 1995** — *Response to Dr. Davis* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **April 1996** — *Diagnosing Porphyria for Labor and Industries Claims* and *Osteoporosis and the Skin* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **June 1996** — *Concerns About Progesterone Cream and Yam Extracts* and *Using Sunlight to Sustain Life* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **August/September 1996** — *Critiquing "Dr. McDaniel Responds" Letter* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **1997** — ***From PMS to Menopause: Female Hormones in Context*** published. The second book — clinician-oriented, drawn from decades of correspondence and consultation. *Townsend Letter* readers are the immediate audience.
- **June 1997** — *Iron: Cumulative Danger* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **Late 1990s** — Newsletter subscription base persists; raypeat.com (the personal site collecting his essays) emerges as an internet outlet, though dating individual essays on the site is difficult. The pre-internet *Ray Peat's Newsletter* serial archive consolidates onto the site over the following decade.

## 2000s: Aspirin, cancer, and the internet community

The 2000s is when Peat's audience expands beyond the *Townsend Letter* readership. The internet does most of the work: forums, raypeat.com, archived PDFs of back issues, and eventually his first regular radio appearances at the end of the decade.

- **June 2000** — *Progesterone Content Corrected* (*Townsend Letter*).
- **January 2, 2000** — *On the Origin of Life* (*Politics & Science* transcript) — the earliest dated transcript in the archive, signaling that the *Politics & Science* radio show with Tim Stark begins around this point. (Date verify against original broadcast; filename encodes 2000-01-02.)
- **January 2, 2001** — *Suppression of Cancer Treatments* (*Politics & Science* transcript). The cancer and aspirin work that becomes the 2006–2007 newsletter cluster is already on the air.
- **April 2006** — *The Dire Effects of Estrogen Pollution* on *NaturoDoc*. The 2006 "ray-peat-newsletter" cluster is when the largest pulse of dated newsletter writing begins.

## 2010s: The Peat-online community

The 2010s is when the Peat-online community emerges as a distinct phenomenon. Forum communities (raypeatforum.com, later /r/raypeat on Reddit), the Generative Energy podcast with Marco Mascia, the IdeaLabs supplement line (Georgi Dinkov), and a wider podcast circuit all consolidate. The *Townsend Letter* publication relationship effectively ends; Peat is now an internet figure.

## 2020s: COVID, final newsletters, and death

- **March 18, 2020** — *Politics & Science: Coronavirus, Immunity and Vaccines, Part 1* — Peat enters the COVID conversation early, with the same framework (endotoxin, serotonin, oxidative metabolism, lactate, learned helplessness) he had been writing about for fifty years.
- **March 24, 2020** — *Politics & Science: Coronavirus, Immunity and Vaccines, Part 2*.
- **March 31, 2020** — *Politics & Science: Coronavirus, Immunity and Vaccines, Part 3*. These three episodes — the last *Politics & Science* episodes in the archive — define late-Peat COVID positioning: skeptical of the prevailing public health narrative, framed in metabolic and political terms.
- **2021** — *Some Effects of Progesterone in Men* — the last dated newsletter in the manifest under *Ray Peat's Newsletter*.
- **2021–2022** — *Ask the Herb Doctor* continues monthly on KMUD through 2022. The archive contains episodes dated through *November 2022* — the month of his death.
- **November 24, 2022** — Ray Peat dies in Eugene, Oregon, at age 86. His daughter and his close circle (Andrew Murray and Sarah Johannessen Murray at *Ask the Herb Doctor*, Tim Stark at *Politics & Science*, Georgi Dinkov, Marco Mascia) confirm the death over the following days. The corpus archive — newsletters, articles, transcripts — passes into the keeping of the Peat-online community.

## Posthumous (2023–present)

- **2023** — Andrew Murray and Sarah Johannessen Murray continue *Ask the Herb Doctor* as a tribute / archival-interpretation show. Generative Energy podcast continues. Bioenergetic.wiki is consolidated by Georgi Dinkov as the canonical archive.
- **2024** — The Generative Energy podcast episodes #19–#32 are re-released or re-transcribed (transcript filenames carry the 2024-01-03 transcription date in this corpus). Numerous reissue interviews and clip compilations circulate on YouTube and X.
- **2024–2025** — The EncycloPEATia project (this encyclopedia), companion plugin, and *The Ray Peat Diet* book begin construction within the Creative Intelligence Agency portfolio. Sources: 180 newsletters, 253 polished transcripts across 14 shows, 244 topical emails, 82 articles, two books, 48 newspaper letters from 1967 to 1991.

## Notes on uncertain dates

Several biographical specifics are not in the corpus and have been flagged here as **approximate** or **verify**:

- **Undergraduate degree and year.** Reed College is widely cited in secondary sources; the corpus does not document it. Year of graduation is approximate.
- **PhD year and exact dissertation title.** Widely reported as 1972 from the University of Oregon under Arnold Soderwall; the corpus contains the 1971 and 1972 journal papers that are dissertation-adjacent but no copy of the dissertation itself.
- **Mexico years' exact span.** Peat lived and taught in Mexico in the 1970s and possibly earlier; the corpus does not pin start or end years.
- **First raypeat.com publication date.** The site collected pre-existing essays; individual posts on the site are often undated.
- **Generative Energy podcast (Marco Mascia) start year.** Episode numbering implies roughly 2018, but the corpus contains transcription-dated files (2024-01-03), not original air dates.
- **First Ask the Herb Doctor episode.** The earliest transcript in the corpus is July 1, 2008; whether the show predates that is uncertain.
- **December 29, 2007 newsletter cluster and July 1–2, 2013 cluster.** These dates likely represent archive upload or republication batches, not original publication dates. The underlying essays may be older.
