# DCA

DCA is the common abbreviation for **dichloroacetate**. See the canonical article: **[[dichloroacetate|Dichloroacetate (DCA)]]**.

The canonical page covers:

- Mechanism (PDK inhibition → PDH reactivation → oxidative phosphorylation resumes)
- The substrate-entry / electron-chain pairing with [[methylene-blue]]
- The Michelakis 2007 *Cancer Cell* paper and the 2010 glioblastoma series
- The patentability problem and the absence of phase III trials
- Peat's positioning in the [[cancer-protocol|cancer adjunct stack]]
- Pharmacology, dosing (10-25 mg/kg/day, pulsed), thiamine cofactor
- Peripheral neuropathy as the dose-limiting toxicity
- Pharmacy access (compounding, international, clinical trials) and jurisdictional notes
- Counter-arguments and data gaps
