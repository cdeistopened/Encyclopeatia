# Thyroid Hormone

Thyroid hormone refers collectively to T4 (thyroxine) and T3 (triiodothyronine)—the hormones produced by the thyroid gland that regulate oxidative [[metabolism]] throughout the body. In Ray Peat's framework, adequate thyroid function is the foundation of metabolic health, and thyroid supplementation is one of the most important interventions for the widespread [[hypothyroidism]] he considered endemic in modern populations.

**For full coverage of thyroid physiology, supplementation, and Peat's framework, see [[thyroid]].**
